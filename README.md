# aiyedun.github.io

Personal website of Rasheed Babatunde Aiyedun, power systems and control engineer.

Live at https://aiyedun.github.io

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole site. Text, layout, fonts and the tab icon are all inside this one file, and the page makes no requests to Google or any other third party.
- `FONT-LICENSES.txt`: licences for the two embedded fonts (SIL Open Font License).

## Optional files

- `photo.jpg`: upload a portrait (about 4:5, for example 800 x 1000 px) and it appears next to your name.
- `cv.pdf`: upload your CV and a "CV (PDF)" link appears beside Email, LinkedIn and GitHub.

## Updating the text

Open `index.html` on GitHub, click the pencil icon, press Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on Mac) to find the sentence you want to change, edit it, and click "Commit changes". The site updates within a minute or two.
